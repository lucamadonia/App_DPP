## Notes for Review

Trackbliss is a professional B2B workspace for Digital Product Passports,
product compliance, supply-chain documentation, returns, and warehouse
operations.

Review access:

- Username: `[ENTER ACTIVE REVIEW ACCOUNT IN APP STORE CONNECT]`
- Password: `[ENTER PASSWORD IN APP STORE CONNECT]`
- Organization: `[ENTER REVIEW TENANT NAME]`

Suggested review path:

1. Sign in and open the Dashboard to view product, batch, document, and
   compliance indicators.
2. Open Products and select `[ENTER REVIEW PRODUCT NAME]`.
3. Open the product's public DPP or scan `[ENTER SAMPLE QR / PUBLIC DPP URL]`.
4. Open Compliance to review checklists and the AI compliance workspace.
5. Open Returns and Warehouse to review the operational modules enabled for the
   demo organization.

No purchase is offered in the iOS app. The billing page is read-only on iOS and
contains no prices, upgrade buttons, checkout links, or calls to purchase on the
web. Existing organizational subscriptions are managed outside the app.

Sign in is available by email/password, email magic link, Google, and Sign in
with Apple. OAuth opens the system browser and returns through the associated
Universal Link `https://trackbliss.eu/auth/callback`.

Account deletion is available without contacting support:

- Admin account: Settings > Account > Danger zone > Delete account
- Customer account: Customer portal > Profile > Delete account

Camera access is used only when the user opens a QR/barcode scanning feature.
Photo access is used only to attach product, return, ticket, or document images,
or to save a user-requested label/report. AI compliance analysis is initiated
by the user and is presented as an assistance tool, not legal advice.

The guest area can be opened from the login screen with “Explore without an
account,” but the active review account is required to inspect tenant features.
